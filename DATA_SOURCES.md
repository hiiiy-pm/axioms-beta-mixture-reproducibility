# Data sources and attribution

The MIT license applies to the research software and its documentation. It does
not replace the rights or terms associated with third-party source data.

The supplied public-data subsets derive from these NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus
studies:

- GSE229715: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE229715
- GSE74167: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE74167

Please cite the original studies and their GEO accessions when reusing the data.
Source URLs, extraction definitions, sample mappings, and saved coefficients are
recorded in `supplementary/data/data_provenance.json`. The source investigators
retain attribution for the original measurements and sample metadata.

The JSON files in `supplementary/results/` contain the archived calculations,
simulations, and diagnostic summaries supporting this study. Please cite this
software release when reusing those results.
